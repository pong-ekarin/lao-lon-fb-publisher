เล่าหลอน — Facebook Publisher

A personal publishing tool maintained by Pong Ekarin for uploading and scheduling Thai ghost-story videos on the เล่าหลอน Facebook Page. This tool is intended for the Page owner's use.

What the app does

  • Uploads Facebook Reels with titles and descriptions.
  • Requests immediate or scheduled publication.
  • Supports optional subtitles and AI-generated media disclosure.
  • Retrieves video processing and publication status.

Facebook authorization

The app uses Facebook authorization to obtain a Page access token. It requests pages_show_list to identify Pages the owner manages, pages_manage_posts to publish content on the selected Page, and pages_read_engagement to read Page content and retrieve video status. Access requires the owner's approval through Facebook's authorization process.
