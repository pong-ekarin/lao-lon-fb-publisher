Privacy Policy

This policy applies to เล่าหลอน — Facebook Publisher, a personal tool maintained by Pong Ekarin. The tool is intended for the Facebook Page owner's use.

Purpose

The app is used by the Page owner to upload and schedule Thai ghost-story videos on the เล่าหลอน Facebook Page.

Facebook data access

The app uses these permissions with the owner's consent:

  • pages_show_list: Identify Pages the owner manages and obtain the access token for the selected Page.
  • pages_manage_posts: Publish and manage content on the selected Page.
  • pages_read_engagement: Read Page content and retrieve video processing and publication information.

How data is used

Page information and the Page access token are used to authorize publishing operations on the selected Page. Videos, titles, descriptions, publishing settings, and optional subtitle files are sent to Meta when the owner initiates an upload. Meta's responses are used to report upload results, processing status, and scheduling information.

Storage and retention

The Page access token is stored locally in the owner's project workspace or supplied through an environment variable. Locally stored tokens are retained for subsequent publishing operations until the owner deletes them. Tokens are not published on this website.

Publishing results may include Page IDs, video IDs, video links, and processing or scheduling information. Any saved copies of these results remain in the owner's workspace until deleted. Uploaded content and its metadata are stored by Meta and can be managed through Facebook or Meta Business Suite.

Data sharing

The app communicates with Meta and Facebook to perform publishing operations. Published content is made available to viewers according to Facebook's publication and visibility settings. The app does not sell Facebook user data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train artificial intelligence models. Facebook data is used only for the publishing functions described here.

Revoking access and deleting local data

Because this is a personal tool, the owner controls its locally stored data and can remove it directly:

  1. Stop using the publishing tool and remove its authorization through the Facebook account's Business Integrations or Apps and Websites settings, as applicable.
  2. Delete the locally stored Page token at .local/publishing/facebook_page_token.txt.
  3. Remove FACEBOOK_PAGE_ACCESS_TOKEN from any environment variables or configuration where it has been saved.
  4. Delete any saved publishing results or other locally retained Facebook data, including copies in backups.

Deleting a local token does not revoke authorization on Facebook. Removing authorization and deleting local data also do not automatically delete videos or posts already published on Facebook. To delete published content, remove it separately through Facebook or Meta Business Suite.
